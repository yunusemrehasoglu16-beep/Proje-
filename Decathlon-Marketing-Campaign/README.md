# Decathlon Türkiye — Marketing Campaign Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview
This portfolio case study analyzes the performance of a simulated digital marketing campaign for Decathlon Türkiye. The project focuses on campaign performance measurement, channel comparison, creative analysis, conversion funnel tracking, and budget optimization.

**Note:** This is an independent portfolio project using simulated data. It is not an official Decathlon campaign and is not affiliated with Decathlon.

## Tools & Technologies
- **SQL Server:** Data exploration, aggregation, and campaign performance analysis
- **Microsoft Power BI:** Interactive dashboard development and visualization
- **DAX:** KPI calculations and measures

## Dashboard Highlights
- **KPI Tracking:** Total Spend, Total Revenue, Purchases, Clicks, Impressions, and ROAS
- **Channel Performance:** Comparison of Google Search, Instagram, and TikTok
- **Performance Over Time:** Daily spend and revenue trends
- **Sport Category Analysis:** Spend and revenue by category
- **Conversion Funnel:** Impressions, clicks, sessions, add to cart, checkout, and purchases
- **Campaign Performance:** Campaign-level spend, revenue, purchases, and ROAS
- **Creative Performance:** Comparison of advertising creatives

## Analytical Objectives
1. Identify the strongest-performing marketing channels.
2. Compare campaign revenue against advertising spend.
3. Evaluate creative performance using clicks and revenue.
4. Identify potential drop-off points in the conversion funnel.
5. Support data-informed budget allocation decisions.

## Data Disclaimer
All campaign data and performance figures in this project are simulated for educational and portfolio purposes. The findings should not be interpreted as actual Decathlon business results.
